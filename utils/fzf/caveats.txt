Shell integration is installed but not enabled. fzf's Ctrl-R history
search and Ctrl-T file widget rebind keys in your shell, so switching
them on is left to you. Add one pair of lines to your shell's rc file:

  zsh   ~/.zshrc
        source ~/.local/share/fzf/key-bindings.zsh
        source ~/.local/share/fzf/completion.zsh

  bash  ~/.bashrc
        source ~/.local/share/fzf/key-bindings.bash
        source ~/.local/share/fzf/completion.bash

  fish  ~/.config/fish/config.fish
        source ~/.local/share/fzf/key-bindings.fish

If you installed with --prefix, those files are under
<prefix>/../share/fzf/ instead.

For vim, add to your .vimrc:  set rtp+=~/.local/share/fzf
